😤 Problem 1: No Live Status
Apps like ChargeZone and Statiq only show whether chargers exist — not whether a specific connector port is free right now. The "Available" badge is often 24 hours old.
📱 Problem 2: Phone While Driving
Drivers must pull out their phone while driving, open 3 different apps, and manually search — which is both dangerous and illegal in India.
🔒 Problem 3: No Reservation
Even if you know a charger is free, someone else can arrive before you and steal your spot. There is no way to lock a connector in advance.
🤑 Problem 4: ICE Vehicle Blocking
Non-electric cars park at charging stations (called ICE-ing). There is no enforcement or economic penalty to stop this from happening.

Section 04 — The Charger Lock System

How the 30-Minute Lock Works

🔄 Charger State Machine — All Possible States
stateDiagram-v2 [*] --> Available: System Starts Available --> Reserved: Driver clicks Reserve in Car\n(OCPP ReserveNow sent) Available --> Charging: Driver plugs in directly\nwithout reservation Reserved --> AccessDenied: Wrong PIN entered\nOR wrong RFID tapped Reserved --> Unlocked: Correct PIN entered\nOR QR code scanned Reserved --> Available: 30-min timer expires\nNo-show fee deducted AccessDenied --> Reserved: Try again (3 attempts max) Unlocked --> Charging: Relay closes\nPower flows to vehicle Charging --> Finishing: Session ends\nSoC reaches 80% or driver stops Finishing --> Available: Cable unplugged

What the Charger Screen Shows in Each State

State LED Color Screen Message Power Status
Available 🟢 Green 60 kW CCS2 Available — Plug in or tap RFID Relay Open · Ready
Reserved 🔵 Blue RESERVED for Hyundai Driver — Enter 6-digit PIN Relay LOCKED · 0 kW
Access Denied 🔴 Red Flash ❌ WRONG PIN — Access Denied. Connector stays locked Relay LOCKED · 0 kW
Unlocked 🟢 Blinking ✅ Identity Verified! Plug in your cable now Awaiting plug-in
Charging 🟢 Solid Charging: 60 kW · SoC: 42% · Time: 28 min left Relay CLOSED · 60 kW flowing
Finishing 🟡 Amber Charging complete at 80%. Please unplug to free connector Idle fee starts in 5 min
🧠 How Physical Lock Works (No Hardware Needed to Demo)
In the real world, the charger's internal microcontroller runs the OCPP state machine. The contactor relay (a high-voltage switch) opens and closes based on OCPP commands. In our software simulator, this is simply a JavaScript boolean variable: relayOpen = true/false. When it is false, the screen shows "0 kW" and "Access Denied" — perfectly mimicking the real hardware behavior for judges.

The Money & Anti-Hogging Rules

✅ Driver Shows Up
The ₹100 UPI pre-authorization hold is credited toward their charging bill. They pay less. It feels like a reward, not a penalty.
❌ Driver No-Shows (30 min expire)
The ₹100 hold is captured as a no-show fee. The charger unlocks automatically and becomes available to others. The CPO and Hyundai split the fee.
🕐 Anti-Hogging (Car Stays Plugged After 80%)
If a car reaches full charge but stays plugged in, idle fees of ₹5/minute kick in. This is the single biggest reason real chargers are blocked in India.
🔧 Charger Breaks During Reservation
If the charger reports a Fault, the reservation is automatically cancelled with a full refund. The driver is notified on the car screen and a nearby free station is suggested.

Section 12 — Be Prepared

Questions Judges Will Ask & Your Answers

Q1: "How does the physical charger know to lock? You cannot install software on real chargers."

✅ Your Answer
"Every modern DC fast charger in India already runs OCPP 1.6 or 2.0 firmware by law — it is required by the Ministry of Power's 2024 BEE guidelines. Our backend acts as the Central System (CSMS) and sends standard ReserveNow commands over WebSocket to the charger's IP address. The charger's own firmware handles the relay lockout. We are not modifying any hardware — we are using the standard protocol the charger already supports."

Q2: "What if the CPO (Tata Power, ChargeZone) refuses to give you API access?"

✅ Your Answer
"That is why Hyundai is the right partner for this. Hyundai already has signed MoUs with ChargeZone, Statiq, and Tata Power for their dealership fast charging network. Hyundai can negotiate API access as part of the partnership terms. For the prototype, we connect to simulated OCPP charger nodes that behave identically to real hardware."

Q3: "Your GNN has 78% accuracy — what about the other 22%? Can we trust it?"

✅ Your Answer
"We always display the prediction as a range with a confidence label, not a hard promise. The UI shows 'Predicted Queue: 0–5 minutes (High Confidence)' or '20–40 minutes (Low Confidence).' This is honest with the driver. We also show the timestamp of the last known live status so the driver knows exactly how fresh the data is."

Q4: "This is just an app. Why is this an in-vehicle experience?"

✅ Your Answer
"The car itself detects the battery level and triggers the system automatically — the driver does not have to open any app. The interface runs on the factory-installed AVNT infotainment touchscreen using Hyundai's ccNC connected car platform. The driver uses the steering wheel controls or voice to confirm the reservation without looking away from the road. This is exactly what BlueLink's roadmap already supports."

Q5: "Is this even legal — deducting money without the user completing a transaction?"

✅ Your Answer
"We use UPI AutoPay mandates or card pre-authorization holds — not actual deductions. The money only moves on a confirmed no-show, exactly like hotel booking cancellation fees which are fully legal under RBI guidelines. We would work with Razorpay or PayU to implement the correct mandate flow before a real launch."
