1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & OVERVIEW
================================================================================
Hyundai SmartReserve has been augmented with AVA (Automated Vehicle Assistant),
an intelligent in-cabin voice co-pilot integrated directly into the 12.3-inch ccNC
infotainment cockpit interface.

All modifications have been implemented strictly on branch "agent", preserving the
core repository and existing features (Leaflet interactive map, 934 BEE stations,
GNN demand forecasting, OCPP 1.6J kiosk digital twin, WebSocket real-time meter
streaming, and Razorpay escrow hold).

Key Enhancements Added on "agent":
- Hands-free voice operation for finding, comparing, and locking EV chargers.
- Dynamic multi-criteria priority engine that evaluates effective charging power,
  distance, queue congestion, tariffs, and historical reliability.
- Vehicle health monitoring covering battery SoC, remaining driving duration,
  average efficiency, individual tyre pressures, component scores, and service alerts.
- Permission tiers (T0-T3) ensuring safety-critical powertrain functions remain
  air-gapped from conversational AI.

3. SMART PRIORITY ENGINE & MULTI-CRITERIA RANKING ALGORITHM
================================================================================
Traditional EV navigation apps sort charging hubs solely by Euclidean or road
distance. In real Indian driving conditions, this frequently directs drivers to low-power
AC chargers or congested bays.

The SmartReserve Priority Engine solves this by calculating:
1. Usable Vehicle Capacity: 72.6 kWh pack (Hyundai Ioniq 5).
2. Effective Charging Power:
     P_effective = min(P_station, Vehicle_Charge_Curve(Arrival_SoC))
   For example, an arrival SoC of 18% allows the Ioniq 5 to pull up to 220 kW, making
   a 120 kW or 150 kW DC Fast site vastly superior to a 30 kW or 50 kW site.
3. Total Time Cost:
     T_total = Drive_Time + Predicted_Queue + Charge_Time_to_80_percent
4. Total Cost:
     Cost = Energy_Needed_kWh * Tariff_per_kWh
5. Weighted Multi-Criteria Score:
     Score = w_dist * Dist_norm + w_time * Time_norm + w_rel * Reliability +
             w_cost * Cost_norm + w_power * Power_norm

Urgency Adaptation:
- Critical (SoC < 10%): High weight on nearest reachable hub to prevent stranding.
- Low (SoC < 20%): High weight on minimum total turnaround time.
- Planning (SoC > 50%): High weight on tariff economy and station amenities.

Ranked Output Categories:
- 🥇 Best Overall: Optimal tradeoff of charging speed, distance, and reliability.
- 📍 Nearest: Minimum driving detour.
- 💰 Cheapest: Lowest tariff per kWh (e.g. state utility hubs at ₹14.50/kWh).
- 🔧 Most Reliable: Stations with >95% historical uptime and verified telemetry.

7. IN-VEHICLE HARDWARE INTEGRATION & ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================
Physical integration into a production vehicle involves four primary components:

1. Telematic Control Unit (TCU):
   - 4G/5G automotive eSIM module (Hyundai BlueLink platform).
   - Secures TLS connection to the cloud backend and CPO endpoints.

2. In-Cabin Cockpit Compute (SoC):
   - High-performance automotive compute (Qualcomm Snapdragon Cockpit 8155 /
     NVIDIA DRIVE Orin).
   - Hosts the Edge SLM (quantised 3B parameter model via ONNX/llama.cpp) for
     offline fallback when driving through remote highway stretches with zero signal.

3. Secure CAN-FD / FlexRay Gateway:
   - Connects to the vehicle's OBD-II / CAN bus via ISO 14229 / UDS diagnostics.
   - Read-only telemetry sniffing:
     * Battery Management System (BMS): Pack voltage, cell temperatures, SoC.
     * Vehicle Control Unit (VCU): Instantaneous Wh/km, speed, odometer.
     * TPMS Receiver: Individual tyre pressure sensor broadcasts.

4. Multi-Microphone Beamforming Array:
   - 4-microphone ceiling array with Hardware DSP for Acoustic Echo Cancellation (AEC).
   - Driver-seat voice isolation to suppress road noise and passenger conversation.

8. SAFETY POLICY & PERMISSION TIERS (T0 - T3)
================================================================================
To guarantee vehicle safety, all actions exposed to AVA are partitioned into
four permission tiers:

- Tier 0: Read-Only Telemetry
  Examples: Battery SoC, range, tyre pressure, service schedule, ETA.
  Policy: Always allowed without driver confirmation.

- Tier 1: In-Cabin Comfort
  Examples: Air conditioning temperature, seat ventilation, ambient lighting, audio volume.
  Policy: Auto-executed with immediate auditory confirmation.

- Tier 2: Consequential & Financial Actions
  Examples: Reserving a 30-min bay (₹200 deposit hold), remote unlocking of charging port.
  Policy: Mandatory verbal or on-screen confirmation ("Reserve slot? haan/yes/ho").
  Moving Vehicle Restriction: Blocked or simplified if vehicle speed > 0 km/h.

- Tier 3: Safety-Critical Powertrain & Dynamics
  Examples: Steering control, friction brakes, emergency braking, drive mode alterations.
  Policy: AIR-GAPPED AND STRICTLY REFUSED 100% OF THE TIME.
  Red-team prompts like "turn off brakes" or "unlock doors while driving" are
  intercepted and refused by the tool registry.

9. END-TO-END USER EXPERIENCE & FLOW WALKTHROUGH
================================================================================
Step 1: Low Battery Alert
Driver is on the road; battery SoC drops below 20% (18.0%).

Step 2: Voice Command Invocation
Driver says "Hey AVA, nearest charging station find karo" (or presses the mic button).

Step 3: Intelligence & Ranking
AVA extracts live GPS (Hyderabad), checks vehicle connector (CCS2), fetches live
stations, calculates CC/CV charging curves, and ranks results.

Step 4: Contextual Response
AVA speaks:
"Aapke paas 3 charging stations hain. Pehla: REIL Hyderabad Fast Hub, 2.8 km door,
122 kW, ~25 min mein 80% tak charge. Best option Station 1 hai — kya isko reserve karu?"

Step 5: Voice Confirmation
Driver responds: "Haan, pehla reserve karo."

Step 6: Reservation & OCPP Lock
AVA executes POST /api/reserve. The backend fires an OCPP 1.6 ReserveNow frame to the
charger kiosk. The physical contactor locks (0 kW), the charger screen turns blue,
and a 6-digit one-time PIN (e.g., 742911) is displayed on the car dashboard.

Step 7: Charger Unlock & Session
Driver arrives at the charger kiosk and enters the PIN. The kiosk verifies the code,
closes the 60/120 kW contactor relay, and begins DC Fast charging with live meter
streaming to the vehicle dashboard.
